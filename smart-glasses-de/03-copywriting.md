# Marketing Copy: Smart Glasses for Germany

## Slogan
Hands-free clarity for German industry—built for precision and privacy compliance.

## Website Copy
### Hero
- **Headline**: Hands-Free Smart Glasses Built for German Workplaces
- **Subheadline**: Streamline inspections, maintenance, and remote support with smart eyewear designed for industrial workflows, data governance, and Works Council readiness.

### Core Value Propositions
1. **Hands-Free Workflow Speed**: Keep both technicians' hands free for equipment checks, guided procedures, and live remote assistance to cut downtime and inspection delays.
2. **Built for German Privacy & Governance**: Designed with clear capture indicators, controllable data handling, and documentation that supports GDPR reviews and Works Council (Betriebsrat) alignment.
3. **Shop-Floor Practicality**: Tailored for real operational environments rather than casual consumer distractions, helping teams standardize procedures across shifts.
4. **Smooth Pilot-to-Rollout Path**: Start with a focused workflow pilot and clear compliance documentation before scaling across sites and partner networks.

### CTA
- **Primary CTA**: Request a German Enterprise Pilot
- **Secondary CTA**: Download the GDPR & Works Council Readiness Brief

## Pitch Deck Points
1. **Problem**: German factories and field teams lose time to manual notes, equipment downtime, and slow on-site expert travel.
2. **Problem**: Consumer smart glasses often clash with workplace privacy rules, trade-secret protection, and Works Council requirements.
3. **Solution**: Purpose-built smart glasses give frontline workers hands-free guidance and remote support during daily operations.
4. **Differentiation**: Unlike consumer lifestyle glasses, our approach prioritizes enterprise governance, clear capture visibility, and compliance readiness.
5. **Differentiation**: Unlike bulky or fragmented setups, we focus on practical workflow adoption and structured pilot validation.
6. **Value**: Faster troubleshooting and more consistent inspections without compromising employee or company data protection.
7. **Value**: A clear, low-friction path from initial pilot to multi-site deployment in Germany.
